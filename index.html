Artist Biography

Essential Booker Overview

In A Nutshell: Concrete Parachute is an Atlanta-based heavy rock quartet striking a balance between heavy grit and melodic clarity. Combining dual lead clean vocals with metalcore intensity, they turn massive riffs and atmospheric soundscapes into high-octane live performances.

Sound & Genre: Emerging from Griffin, Georgia, Concrete Parachute delivers an impact-driven blend of heavy melodic rock. Fusing heavy guitar riffage with clean, dual-lead vocal hooks, the four-piece captures heavy rock aggression while remaining catchy and radio-accessible.

Influences & Lineup: Influenced by Chevelle, early A Day to Remember, and Breaking Benjamin, the band features co-lead vocalists Chad Askew (lead guitar) and Brandon Halvorsen (rhythm guitar), grounded by Josh Hollums (bass) and Michael Hayes (drums).

Key Achievements & Operations: The band travels with a fully self-contained 16-channel IEM split rack, guaranteeing clean stage volume and a fast 15-minute patch time for festival crews. Drummer Michael Hayes brings extensive experience from Reaching for Resolve.

Past Shows & Upcoming Dates: Having built regional momentum across North Georgia, Concrete Parachute is actively booking festival slots, direct support dates, and weekend runs across Georgia and the Southeast for 2027/2028.

Stage Spec & Tech Rider

15-Minute Festival Patch Guarantee

Download Stage Plot (PDF)

1. Low Stage Volume

No live bass cabs on stage. Guitars run direct/amplified DI to FOH; Bass is 100% DI.

2. Isolated Splitter Rack

Band provides complete 16-channel analog rack split with pre-labeled XLR tails ready for FOH stage snake.

3. Power & IEMs

Requires ONE standard 120V / 20A circuit at drum riser. Zero stage wedges needed (100% IEM band).
